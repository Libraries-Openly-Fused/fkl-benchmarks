# notes: FKL HIP vs RPP and hand-written kernels; source: seeded noise (LCG, seed 12345); RPP 3.2.0 (ROCm package amdrocm-rpp), rppt tensor API, HIP back-end; rpp_crop_batch: the N crops copied into one dense batch (one hipMemcpy2DAsync per crop), then rppt_resize_mirror_normalize (BILINEAR, one ROI XYWH per crop: the whole crop, source U8 NHWC with batch stride CROP_H * CROP_W * 3, destination F32 NCHW, mirror off) and rppt_channel_permute (F32 NCHW); RPP has its own frame (dense hipMalloc) and its own hipStream_t; output widths are multiples of 8; FKL: fk::PerThreadRead::build(crop2D views).then(Resize) -> VectorReorder<float3, 2, 1, 0> -> Mul -> Sub -> Div -> TensorSplit, TransformDPP<GPU_AMD, TF::DISABLED>, the crop views and every operation built in every timed call; hand_batch: the hand-written fused kernel of E1c (handwritten-cuda/e1_preproc.cu: handPreproc) ported to HIP (__grid_constant__ dropped), one launch with 32x8 threads per block, ASYMMETRIC sampling; its own frame (hipMallocPitch, pitch 11520 bytes; FKL's frame: pitch 11520 bytes), output (hipMalloc) and hipStream_t; build = the crop pointers (its parameter block, 8 bytes per crop), compute = the launch, hipGetLastError and the stream synchronisation; hand_padded: E1c's diagnostic handPreprocPadded (crop descriptors, the parameter block padded to the size of FKL's read IOp), build = the descriptors; hand_roi: the hand-written kernel of E4 (opencv-cuda/batched-preprocessing/e4_preprocessing.cu: handPreprocRoi) for crops of any location and size, build = the crop rectangles and its CPU function handRoiParameters (plain C++, no library: per crop the pointer, width, height, pitch and resize factors, 32 bytes); the three hand-written kernels share their frame, output and stream, and their compute part has no release of crop views; wall clock on the host including the stream synchronisation; build_median_ms and compute_median_ms are the medians of the two parts of a run: FKL build = the crop views and the operations (host), compute = executeOperations and the synchronisation (no synchronisation between them); rpp_crop_batch build = the copies and a stream synchronisation, compute = the ROIs, the two RPP calls and a stream synchronisation, so its total has one synchronisation more than rpp_crop_batch_unsplit, which has no parts (nan); the median of the runs is not the sum of the medians of the parts; warm-up: 0 runs, at least 0.0 s and until the mean time of two consecutive windows of 0.25 s differs by at most 2 percent (cap 10 s; the columns warmup_s and warmup_steady record it); sclk_*, mclk_*, pcie_gts_* and pcie_width_*: the graphics clock, the memory clock (MHz) and the PCIe link speed (GT/s) and width (lanes) at the start of the warm-up, at the start of the timed runs and after them (-1: not readable); the variants of a point are timed in a rotated order: the first one is variant ((index of the point + E5_ROTATE_OFFSET = 0) mod the number of variants)
