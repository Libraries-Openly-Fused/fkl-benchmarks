FKL notes:
- **For a 3-channel pipeline,** only LinearFilterDPP (user weights or box), MorphologyDPP (erode/dilate/open/close/box blur) and, with a selection IOp that does not exist yet, MedianFilterDPP take float3 (or uchar3 for Min/Max). The four *Quad DPPs are single-channel only.
- **No FKL Gaussian filter.** A Gaussian is LinearFilterDPP with Gaussian coefficients from the user. Matching RPP or OpenCV then means matching their weights exactly (see Part 2).
- **Rounding to u8 inside the chain:** SaturateCast<float3,uchar3> (nearbyint: ties to even, saturating). Then Cast<uchar3,float3> (algorithms/basic_ops/cast.h) gives the float input the DPPs need. FKL can therefore reproduce a baseline that rounds to u8 after the resize, if the baseline also rounds ties to even. Part 2 says how RPP rounds.
- **All the DPPs border relative to details.width/height,** that is the image produced by the read chain (the resized image), not the source frame.
