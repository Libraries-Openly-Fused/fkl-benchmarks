what is measured: host time, not GPU time. The time the CPU spends in the calls that enqueue the work (std::chrono::steady_clock around the OpenCV-CUDA calls of one iteration and around the one cvGS::executeOperations call of the same iteration, no synchronisation inside the timed region), 100 iterations after a warm-up pass. It measures the launch cost of the GPU pipelines; it is not the CPU back-end of the library
speedup = host time of the baseline / host time of the fused call: the column 'Mean Speedup', the mean over the 100 iterations of the per-iteration ratio; the ratio of the two mean times is lower by 0.5 % to 2.7 % over the batch sizes
the OpenCV-CUDA side of this experiment is the per-crop loop: for each crop on one stream convertTo, resize, multiply, cvtColor (3 channels), subtract, divide, split = 7 enqueued calls per crop, 7 x batch in total (benchmark_CPU_OpenCV_vs_cvGS.cu); it is not the stacked 'opencv_batch' formulation of E4, which runs 3 x batch + 4 calls (e4_preprocessing.cu) and is not in this experiment
